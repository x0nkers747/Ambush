## What is missing (not faked)

- **Rifle locomotion with correct proportions**: KayKit's rifle clips are chibi; their arm poses do
  not hold a rifle on a realistic body. The prototype holds the rifle procedurally (weapon placed from
  the shoulder and aim, hands by IK) over the idle clip's upper body.
- **Prone idle, stand→prone, prone→stand** on the stomach: none in any CC0/CC BY source found.
  The prototype holds the crawl clip still for prone idle and crossfades postures over the
  simulation's posture-change window — labelled as placeholders.
- **Kneeling (one knee down)**: only a deep crouch (UAL `Crouch_Idle`) is available.
- **Rifle reload on a realistic rig**: KayKit `Ranged_2H_Reload` is retargetable but chibi; the
  prototype animates the support hand procedurally (magwell → pouch → magwell).
- **M249 SAW**: no openly licensed model or clip. A box-built placeholder, labelled "M249
  PLACEHOLDER", uses the rifle hold with different sockets.
- **Turn-in-place clips without root rotation**: the UAL mocap turns rotate the root; the prototype
  steps the walk clip by the turned angle instead.
