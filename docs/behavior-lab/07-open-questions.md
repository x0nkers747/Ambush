# Behavior Lab 07: open questions

- Status: for decision
- Date: 2026-10-14
- Part of: [Behavior Lab research](README.md)

Each question has a recommendation. Those marked **(needs your decision)** change scope, the
game's behaviour or doctrine choices. The others are technical, and we will proceed with the
recommendation unless you say otherwise.

| #   | Question                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Recommendation                                                                                                                          | Rationale                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Q1  | Where does the engine live: in the simulation kernel (`src/sim/behavior`) or a new layer?                                                                                                                                                       | **Kernel.**                                                                                                                             | It changes authoritative positions inside the tick and must be hashed, snapshotted and recorded. A separate layer would duplicate the kernel or bypass its rules (03 §3).                                      |
| Q2  | Tick-rate independence: the engine integrates in fixed 120 Hz substeps. Rates that divide 120 are bit-identical; other allowed rates (e.g. 50, 240) are deterministic but only approximately equal. Accept, or restrict the allowed tick rates? | **Accept.** Keep the 1–240 Hz range; document that behaviour results are identical only for divisors of 120; test others within 0.05 m. | The game runs at 30 Hz, which divides 120. Restricting the rate range is a kernel-wide change for no practical gain. A 240 Hz substep would cost 2× for nothing.                                               |
| Q3  | **(needs your decision)** Role 4 of the Marine fire team: keep the 1991 "assistant automatic rifleman" (the current template, MCWP 3-11.2), or move to the 2020 "grenadier" (MCRP 3-10A.4)?                                                     | **Keep the current template**, and let formation templates accept either role for that slot.                                            | The geometry is identical in both manuals' figures (02 §6.2). Changing the roster changes loadouts and the game's look, which is outside this initiative.                                                      |
| Q4  | **(needs your decision)** Default pace: the game moves everyone at 1.5 m/s. Doctrine's 4 km/h road rate implies about 1.33 m/s while walking (02 §6.4); pedestrian free speed is about 1.34 m/s.                                                | **Lab default 1.4 m/s**, configurable. The game keeps 1.5 m/s until M7.                                                                 | 1.4 m/s sits within the research ranges and leaves catch-up headroom to 1.8 m/s without running.                                                                                                               |
| Q5  | Clearance field: whole-map at terrain load, or lazy tiles?                                                                                                                                                                                      | **Whole map first**, measured on 5 km fixtures in M4; lazy tiles if it costs over 300 ms or 16 MB.                                      | It is simpler, deterministic either way, and the estimate (50–150 ms, 12.5 MB) is within budget, but unmeasured.                                                                                               |
| Q6  | The 3D view takes a whole `Exercise`. Narrow its option type to what it reads, or give the lab an adapter?                                                                                                                                      | **Narrow the type** (a type-only change).                                                                                               | The game passes `Exercise` as before. An adapter would fake an API surface and drift from it.                                                                                                                  |
| Q7  | **(needs your decision)** Slope-dependent speed (none today, ADR 0008)?                                                                                                                                                                         | **Not in M1–M4.** In M5, offer a Tobler-style factor for unit tasks only, as an option to approve.                                      | It changes movement realism and the game's timing, and needs calibration. Fixed substeps remove ADR 0008's technical objection.                                                                                |
| Q8  | **(needs your decision)** Ship the lab in the production build (lazily loaded, like the demo) or dev-only?                                                                                                                                      | **Ship it**, lazily loaded at `#behavior-lab`.                                                                                          | It costs players nothing unless opened, and lets the deployed site demonstrate behaviours for review. The terrain lab was dev-data-only because of its large data files, which the Behavior Lab does not need. |
| Q9  | Leader casualty: who takes over and does the formation change?                                                                                                                                                                                  | **Out of scope now.** The formation does not depend on any one Marine (03 §5). Succession comes with combat behaviours.                 | Avoids inventing doctrine before combat integration.                                                                                                                                                           |
| Q10 | An individual order to a Marine who is part of a moving unit: release him from the task, or override temporarily?                                                                                                                               | **Release** (ADR 0022's rule: individual edits detach). A later REGROUP order brings him back.                                          | One clear rule; no hidden state; consistent with planning formations.                                                                                                                                          |
| Q11 | **(needs your decision)** Should the game's BLUEFOR planning eventually issue unit orders (one destination for a squad) instead of per-Marine destinations?                                                                                     | **Decide after M3**, from the lab results.                                                                                              | It changes the game's workflow, which the brief excludes from this initiative.                                                                                                                                 |
| Q12 | Facing during movement: always toward travel, or observation sectors (flank Marines facing out)?                                                                                                                                                | **Toward travel in M1–M6.** Sectors are a later behaviour (the characters already support walking while facing elsewhere).              | Keeps the first experiment about movement. Sectors are tactical.                                                                                                                                               |
| Q13 | Automatic formation changes: may the task switch to compressed or file by itself, or only when commanded?                                                                                                                                       | **Automatic for compression and file** (driven by clearance, with hysteresis). **Commanded for the template** (wedge, column, line).    | Doctrine has leaders change formation as terrain demands (02 §6.3). Narrowing to pass a gap is not a tactical choice.                                                                                          |
| Q14 | **(needs your decision)** Default fire-team wedge for Marines: the Marine figure (rifleman at the point) or the Army one (team leader at the point)?                                                                                            | **USMC figure** (MCRP 3-10A.4 Fig 1-12) for Marines; the Army wedge available as a template.                                            | Ambush! models Marines; both are data.                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Q15 | Catch-up speed: walk faster only (≤ 1.3 × pace), or allow jogging?                                                                                                                                                                              | **Walk faster only** in M1–M6, with the anchor giving way instead.                                                                      | Running stragglers look wrong in a patrol, and the animation has no running strafe clips (01 §9).                                                                                                              |
| Q16 | Spacing defaults beyond the 10 m individual interval: team spacing in a squad, squad spacing in a platoon.                                                                                                                                      | **Team spacing 20 m and squad spacing 50 m, labelled tuning [E]**, adjustable in the lab.                                               | Doctrine gives no numbers here (02 §6.4). These sit inside the inferred ranges and under the 300 m platoon limit.                                                                                              |
| Q17 | The lab's replay re-simulates (with hash checks) rather than storing frames like the game's AAR. Acceptable?                                                                                                                                    | **Yes.** The game's frame-based AAR is tested separately (05 §5).                                                                       | It keeps long runs small, and the lab exists to prove determinism.                                                                                                                                             |
| Q18 | Behaviour parameters as a versioned, fingerprinted config file (`behavior-v1.json`) in the world, like `combat-v1.json`?                                                                                                                        | **Yes.**                                                                                                                                | Makes tuning traceable, fingerprints the world, and lets the lab sweep parameters without code changes.                                                                                                        |
